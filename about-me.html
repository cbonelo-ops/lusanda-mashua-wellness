Hi there

I’m Lusanda Mashua — a registered wellness counsellor and the heart behind LM Wellness, a space where healing is relational, embodied, and rooted in the real-life context of who we are.

I hold a Bachelor of Applied Social Science (BAppSocSci) in Psychology and Counselling from The South African College of Applied Psychology (SACAP). My professional training is trauma-informed, person-centred, and shaped by the understanding that we are not isolated beings. Our emotional health is affected by our relationships, nervous systems, histories, and the environments we live in.

I’m also a creative at heart — a writer, facilitator, and storyteller. I’m a mother of three, navigating the beautiful, messy reality of family life with deep love and presence. These roles have shaped how I hold space: with honesty, compassion, and curiosity. I'm passionate about strength training, immersing myself in nature, and creating spaces where people feel safe enough to be real. Whether it's joy or grief, tenderness or tension, I believe that every part of your story deserves to be witnessed with care.

My work honours both your inner world and the outer forces that influence; from attachment styles and family patterns to broader systems like race, class, culture, and history. Whether we connect in a one-on-one session, a group setting, or during a retreat, the process is collaborative. You bring your story and your pace. Together, we shape a journey that fits who you are and how you process.

At the core of LM Wellness is the belief that healing is not about fixing yourself. It’s about remembering what’s already whole and awakening the agency that has always lived within you.
