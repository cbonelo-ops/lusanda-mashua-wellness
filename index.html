Journeying through pain, and integrating your experiences, messy and complex, is always a risk. What it also can be is a surprising adventure where the pain we can’t face is finally engaged in safety so that wounds turn into wisdom. I don’t have the answers to fix you, I too, am a learner. We don’t navigate our shadows with those who have not faced and named their shadows. That’s where I come in, another growing human, here to explore the pain points and possibilities of our story one act of courage at a time.
