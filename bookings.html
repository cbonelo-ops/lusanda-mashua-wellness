One-on-One Counseling Sessions

You don’t have to carry it all alone.
This is a space for you to pause, process, and find your footing again—gently and without pressure. I offer one-on-one wellness counselling sessions for adults navigating emotional overwhelm, grief, burnout, or life transitions. These are not clinical therapy sessions, but guided, ethical, and compassionate conversations that help you reconnect with your inner clarity, courage, and peace.

As a registered Wellness Counsellor (ASCHP), I work within a scope that honours your dignity, preserves your agency, and supports your mental, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing. My practice is trauma-conscious, agency-nurturing, culturally sensitive, and grounded in holistic care.

What’s

in a 50 minute session?

  • A guided check-in to help you ground and be present
  • Exploration of the presenting concerns (emotional distress, relational tensions, burn-out, spiritual struggles, environmental pressures etc)
  • Supportive tools drawn from wellness psychology (e.g. values clarification, emotion mapping, reflective listening, body-mind awareness)
  • Collaborative goal setting for the week ahead or season you’re in
  • Confidential space that honours your story and offers non-judgmental support
