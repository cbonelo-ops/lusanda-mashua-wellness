One-Day Wellness Workshop Enquiry

This one-day, trauma-informed workshop helps your team or community reconnect with themselves, each other, and the values that make work or service meaningful. Whether you're a corporate HR lead, ministry director, or organizational manager, this offering is built to honour the whole person—not just the productivity.

Blending emotional intelligence, embodied regulation tools, and practical relational skills, each session is tailored to your context—whether you’re navigating burnout, miscommunication, leadership challenges, or culture shifts.

We work with you to co-create a space of reflection, renewal, and reconnection—for people to feel seen, supported, and ready to show up with clarity and care.
